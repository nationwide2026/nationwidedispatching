Our Services

We provide full-service dispatching for carriers across the United States. Our platform includes real-time GPS tracking, ELD monitoring, load assignment, RPM calculations, and automated driver status updates.

Whether you're a single owner-operator or a fleet with multiple drivers, Nationwide Dispatching gives you the tools to stay organized and efficient.

Driver Tools

Drivers can log in to update their status, view assigned loads, and automatically send GPS location updates to dispatch. Our mobile-friendly interface ensures drivers stay connected on the road.

Dispatcher Tools

Dispatchers can monitor all drivers in real time, assign loads, update RPM, track delivery progress, and manage HOS compliance. The dashboard provides a complete overview of your fleet.

Why Choose Us?

Our dispatching system is built for speed, reliability, and accuracy. With integrated GPS tracking and automated updates, you always know where your drivers are and how your loads are progressing.
