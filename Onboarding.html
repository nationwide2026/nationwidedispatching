Carrier Onboarding
6‑Step Dispatch Activation
Follow the steps below to activate dispatching with Nationwide Dispatching. Once completed, your onboarding details will be sent directly to nationwidedispatching@outlook.com.
Step 01 — Requirements
Minimum requirements needed before dispatch activation.

Active MC or DOT

Your authority must be active and in good standing.

ELD System

Motive, Samsara, Garmin, or equivalent required.

Equipment Ready

Dry Van · Reefer · Flatbed · Hot Shot · Power Only.

Valid Insurance

Liability + Cargo coverage meeting broker requirements.

Step 02 — Dispatch Agreement
Review and accept the dispatch terms before activation.

No Forced Dispatch

You approve every load before booking.

Transparent Pricing

Simple percentage‑based pricing with no hidden fees.

Carrier‑First Approach

We prioritize your lanes, home time, and RPM goals.

Step 03 — Driver & Truck Details
Provide basic information about your drivers and equipment.

Driver Info

Name · Phone · Email · Experience.

Truck Details

Year · Make · Model · VIN · Trailer type.

Operating Preferences

Home time · Preferred lanes · Max daily miles.

Step 04 — Billing Setup
Ensure your billing information is ready for broker setup.

Factoring Info

MC · Contact · Email · NOA if applicable.

Payment Terms

Net terms · Quick pay · Factoring requirements.

Broker Setup

We handle packets, COIs, W‑9s, and setup documents.

Step 05 — Compliance Review
Final compliance checks before dispatch activation.

Safety Rating

Ensure your DOT safety rating is acceptable.

Insurance Verification

Confirm liability + cargo coverage meets standards.

ELD Compliance

Ensure logs and duty status are properly maintained.

Step 06 — Activation
Submit your onboarding form below to activate dispatching.
